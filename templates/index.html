What This Ecosystem Is

  • Modular LEGO Carriers — Custom KiCad PCBs sized to LEGO stud pitch (12×6 and 8×6 grids with 4.8mm clearance).
  • Low-Power E-Paper Displays — Fast partial refresh (<0.3s) for crisp desk clocks and ambient status without screen flicker.
  • Multi-Sensor Environmental Telemetry — Bosch BME280/680, Sensirion SGP30/40 VOC, and TEMT6000 ambient light sensors.
  • 41 Production ESPHome Nodes — Running on ESP32-C3, ESP32-C6, ESP32-S3, ESP8266 with encrypted native API.

Design Philosophy

Zero cloud lock-in. Everything operates 100% locally through ESPHome's encrypted native API and local MQTT brokers. Modular LEGO mounting allows instant prototyping, infinite color choices, wall mounts, and desk stands without brittle 3D-printed clips.
